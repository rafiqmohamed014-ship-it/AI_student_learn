Privacy Policy

Last updated:

1. What we store

Kalvi AI stores only what is needed to make the product work:

  • Account data — your name, email, education level, course, preferred subjects and study goal.
  • Learning data — lesson completions, quiz answers and scores, study-session minutes, notes, flashcards, study plans and AI conversations.
  • Preferences — theme, notification and AI settings.

We never store your password. In local mode no password exists at all. In Supabase mode, passwords are hashed by Supabase Auth and are never readable by this application.

2. Where your data lives

By default everything is stored locally in your own browser using localStorage. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. If you configure Supabase, your data is stored in your Supabase project, protected by Row Level Security policies so that a signed-in student can only ever read and write their own rows.

3. What is sent to the AI provider

When you use an AI feature (chat, quiz generation, study plan, summarize, flashcards), the relevant text is sent from your server (/api/ai) to whichever AI provider you configured. That request contains your message, your study profile (level, course, subjects) and, where relevant, the lesson or note you are working on.

Your API key is read from a server-side environment variable and is never exposed to the browser. We do not add analytics, advertising or third-party tracking scripts.

4. Deleting your data

You can erase everything at any time:

  • Local mode: Settings → Data → Delete all local data, or clear site data in your browser.
  • Supabase mode: delete your auth user in the Supabase dashboard; the foreign-key cascades remove every related row.

5. Sharing

We do not sell or share personal data. The only third party that receives your content is the AI provider you configured, which processes requests on its own terms.

6. Children

This is an educational tool intended for secondary school students and above. If a student is under 13, a parent or guardian should supervise its use and agree to the Terms.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated as the product evolves. The date at the top always reflects the current version.
